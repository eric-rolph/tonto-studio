What a close match means

Free-running oscillators, noise, component tolerances and the recording chain prevent a universal sample-for-sample match. A similar spectrum alone also does not establish the same circuit or patch.

The lab aligns detected onsets, then searches ±15 ms for a local sample offset. It does not stretch time. Waveform error uses least-squares gain and polarity; spectrum and envelope use RMS gain. Lower error is better, but there is no “authenticity percentage.” Periodicity-based pitch estimates can choose the wrong octave on FM or percussion.

Fitting minimizes spectrum error plus 0.65 × envelope error at the selected sample rate. It changes checked controls only, not routing, note, gate or hardware model. A fit to one note is not validation across the keyboard.

For calibration, record dry 24-bit WAV at 48 or 96 kHz with the exact module revision, panel photo, cables, CV levels, tuning, note and gate duration. Capture repeated notes, several pitches and several drive levels. Keep effects and the microphone/tape stages as separate comparisons.

The current cabinets remain approximations. Easel and 200e recordings are related references for our Buchla 200 model; Synthi AKS is related to VCS3. The Eurorack engine is not a Plaits, Rings or Clouds firmware port. Artist recordings and unknown patches are listening targets, not calibration ground truth.
